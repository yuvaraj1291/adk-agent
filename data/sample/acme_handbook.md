# Zephyrine Robotics Employee Handbook (fictional sample data)

## Company overview

Zephyrine Robotics was founded in 2019 in Tromsø, Norway, by Ingrid Valtersen. The company builds autonomous underwater drones for inspecting offshore wind turbine foundations. Its flagship product is the Kelpie-7 drone.

## Paid time off

Full-time employees receive 31 days of paid vacation per year. Unused vacation days can be carried over until March 31 of the following year. Employees also get a "Polar Night Day": one extra day off taken any day in December.

## Remote work policy

Employees may work remotely up to three days per week. Every Wednesday is a mandatory in-office "Dive Day" when teams test drones in the Tromsø harbour test pool.

## Expense policy

Travel must be booked through the internal tool "Seaplane". Meals while travelling are reimbursed up to 650 NOK per day. Expenses above 10,000 NOK require approval from a department head.

## Kelpie-7 specifications

The Kelpie-7 has a maximum depth rating of 450 metres, a battery life of 9 hours, and a top speed of 4.2 knots. It uses a sonar system called "EchoWeave" and ships with the "Tidewatch" inspection software.
